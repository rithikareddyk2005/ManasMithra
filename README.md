Overview

ManasMitra AI is an AI-powered personal well-being companion developed in alignment with United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 3 (SDG 3): Good Health and Well-Being. It aims to make everyday self-care more accessible, engaging, and personalized by combining artificial intelligence, mindfulness, healthy habit tracking, and interactive wellness experiences.

The platform helps users become more aware of their emotional state, manage everyday stress, build healthier routines, and strengthen social connections. Its AI-powered assistant offers supportive conversations and practical suggestions, while features such as the Interactive Zen Sanctuary, Digital Zen Mode, Kindness Tracker, Mental Health Solutions Studio, Somatic Body Scan, and Personalized Well-Being Dashboard encourage users to prioritize their well-being through small, meaningful actions.

ManasMitra AI also offers five customizable visual themes, guided relaxation exercises, soundscapes, journaling activities, preventive well-being insights, and educational resources to create a calming and engaging user experience.

Our Mission

To empower individuals to nurture their mental, emotional, physical, and social well-being through accessible AI-powered guidance, mindful practices, and sustainable lifestyle habits.

Our Vision

To create a supportive digital environment where well-being becomes a natural part of everyday life, contributing to a healthier, more mindful, and connected society.
